The back of the print, and the raw print itself

Your rule: while the prints fly past on the rail they are face down, so the back is what a student stares at for eight seconds. It has to be beautiful on its own and it has to carry the tier colour somewhere, so rarity reads while it spins. The front is a raw print: the photograph in a thin white border, no grade, no label.
