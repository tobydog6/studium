The pull, start to finish

Press a tier and watch the whole run with the sound on: the rail powers up, the prints fly, the ticks thin out from a rattle to single clicks, the near miss, and the landing. The landing is scaled by tier, from a quiet settle for a Common to a blackout and an eclipse for a Mythic. The prints on the rail are raw, no grade on them yet; the finish shows on the one you pull. What happens, by tier

  • Every pull: tubes flicker on down the rail, the strip lurches and goes, blur and streaks at full speed, the pointer ticks each print past, a long slow-down, the near miss, then the rest of the rail dims and the pull comes forward.
  • Common: a quiet settle and the name, small.
  • Uncommon: a foil sweep across the print.
  • Rare: the print spins once to show its back, glints fly.
  • Epic: a starburst behind it, sparks, the tier colour floods the room.
  • Legendary: slow motion, a gold flood, a rolling shine, embers rising, the name slams in with a shock ring.
  • Mythic: blackout. Then an eclipse corona, the print appears in prism black, the 1 OF 1 stamp, a white flash.
  • Flip reveal (the Hearthstone way, a knob below): the rail still runs and stops, but the pulled print comes to the centre face down, the tier colour swells behind it for a couple of seconds, then it turns over with a burst of light and the tier moment follows.
